# Recovery Intelligence Platform V1–V4

This repository is the primary implementation of the Recovery Intelligence SaaS.

## V1
- Multi-tenant organization/workspace model
- Users, roles and permissions
- Case and borrower management
- Recovery workflow
- Field operations
- Maps
- Communications and documents
- Analytics, compliance and audit

## V2
- Authorized/public source registry
- Search planning
- HTTP and permitted browser acquisition
- Parsing, OCR and document extraction
- Evidence provenance and content hashes
- Deduplication
- Entity resolution
- Address/contact intelligence
- Deterministic confidence scoring
- Investigation jobs and source health

## V3
- Public government/regulatory/business/document intelligence
- Targeted worldwide public-web discovery
- Search recursion with bounded depth
- Address clustering and timelines
- Contradiction and temporal analysis
- Human review queues
- Evidence graph expansion

## V4
- Public professional/profile discovery
- Public social/professional profile correlation
- Employment candidates and employment timelines
- Company discovery/resolution
- Public company locations and public roles
- Evidence-supported organizational relationships
- Person/company/profile graph

## Safety and governance
All investigations are case-scoped and authorization-scoped. The platform only uses public or explicitly authorized sources. It must not bypass authentication, CAPTCHAs, source restrictions, rate limits, private profiles, private messages, restricted government systems, leaked databases, or covert device tracking. Scraped content is untrusted input and must not control AI tools or permissions.

Every finding must retain source provenance and distinguish observed, correlated, inferred, human-confirmed and authorized-verified states.
